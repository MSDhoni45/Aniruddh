Ask a chatbot to help you break the law, and it often will.

Researchers at Tsinghua University built 5,747 illegal requests from real court cases and tested ten popular chatbots.

These were earlier models, not the newest ones.

Still, a chatbot's safety shouldn't depend on who it thinks you are.
