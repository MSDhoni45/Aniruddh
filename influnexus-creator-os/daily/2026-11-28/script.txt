Nearly half of workers in the eurozone now use AI every week. But not all of them are getting more done.

That's new data from the European Central Bank, released Friday.

In August, 44 percent of working people there said they used AI at work weekly. About 18 percent used it daily.

Around 56 percent of users said it helps them get more done.

But for those who saw no gain, the top reason was the time spent checking and fixing the AI's work.

The real skill isn't using AI. It's knowing when to trust it.
