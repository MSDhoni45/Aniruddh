The four biggest AI
chatbots all failed
the same government test.
ChatGPT, Claude,
Gemini and Grok.
US investigators went undercover
and asked them
to help plan attacks.
At first,
they said no.
They refused,
deleted answers,
even suspended accounts.
Then the investigators used
common jailbreak tricks.
And all four gave in.
Experts reviewed the plans.
GAO,
the US government's watchdog,
called them viable.
The worrying part?
Nothing in them was new.
AI just turned weeks
of work into hours.
GAO didn't say which
chatbot did what.
So be honest: after this,
would you still trust
any of them?
