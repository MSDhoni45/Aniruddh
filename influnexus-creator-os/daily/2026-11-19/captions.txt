This man lost
his voice to ALS.
A brain implant
gave it back.
This is Casey Harrell.
Sensors in his brain
catch signals when he
tries to speak.
Software turns them into words,
read out in a voice
trained on his old recordings.
This isn't a lab demo.
A Nature Medicine study says
he used it at home
for over 3,800 hours,
with no researchers there.
Nearly two million words.
He kept working full-time.
Most tech headlines
are about replacing people.
This one gave
a man his voice back.
