2002 mein ek game
aayi thi jo 1986
mein set thi.
Neon lights,
Hawaiian shirts,
aur Miami se inspired
ek sheher: Vice City.
Hero tha Tommy Vercetti,
aur uski awaaz thi Hollywood
actor Ray Liotta ki.
Lekin is game ka asli
jaadu tha uska radio.
Gaadi mein baitho,
aur 80s ke asli
gaane bajne lagte the.
Zyada-tar music licensed
tha: real songs,
real artists.
Isiliye Vice City
ek game kam,
aur ek time machine
zyada lagti thi.
Aur sach batao,
aapne bhi kabhi mission
chhod ke sirf gaadi
chalayi hai na,
bas gaane sunne ke liye?
Vice City ki aapki sabse
pehli yaad kya hai?
