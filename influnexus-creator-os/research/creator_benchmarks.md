# Creator Benchmarks — Influnexus competitor research

Observation date: 2026-11-18. Status: desk research from web-search results only. No video was watched.

## 0. Access limitations (read first)

- The research sandbox could not open instagram.com, youtube.com, x.com, tiktok.com or analytics sites. Two direct page fetches (vidiq.com, creatordb.app) failed with DNS errors, so direct fetching was abandoned.
- Everything below comes from **web-search result summaries and snippets** (US index): 52 searches. The underlying pages were not opened, so every figure is "as reported by a search result citing that URL", not read off the live profile.
- **No video or audio was observed.** For every creator, the following are `NOT OBSERVED (video inaccessible)`: opening words, words per minute, shot length, cut frequency, zoom frequency, caption font/size/colour, music use, on-screen framing.
- Follower numbers come from third-party trackers (CreatorDB, vidIQ, Social Blade, Qoruz, Socialveins, SponsorRadar, OutlierKit and similar). Trackers disagree, lag, and sometimes merge platforms. Treat every number as an estimate.
- One search result reports that Meta removed fake/inactive accounts in or around May 2026 and most large accounts lost followers. Instagram counts dated before that may be overstated. Source: https://www.socialpilot.co/instagram-marketing/most-followers-on-instagram (as summarised by search; not opened).
- Per-video data for the last 30 days was **not surfaced for any creator**. No short-form video could be identified with URL + metrics.

### Evidence labels

- **MEASURED** — a number or fact shown in a search result and attributed to a named source URL. Still second-hand (tracker or press), never read from the platform itself.
- **THIRD-PARTY** — someone else's analysis, summary or interpretation of a creator's method.
- **HYPOTHESIS** — our editorial inference. Not evidence.

## 5. Weekly update checklist (for a human or a session with Instagram/YouTube access)

Pick the 5 most recent reels/shorts per creator plus their top 3 of the last 90 days. Log one CSV row per video in `creator_video_samples.csv` with `evidence_level=MEASURED` only when read directly off the platform.

**A. Metadata**
- [ ] Video URL, platform, publish date and time (IST), duration in seconds.
- [ ] Views (plays), likes, comments, shares/sends, saves if visible; follower count on the observation date.
- [ ] Topic in five words; format (face-cam, screen demo, b-roll voiceover, split-screen, AI avatar).
- [ ] Caption text length; number of hashtags; pinned comment or CTA.
- [ ] Audio: original voice, trending sound, or music bed.

**B. First three seconds**
- [ ] Exact opening words, transcribed verbatim (first sentence), and the language of each word.
- [ ] First frame: face, screen, object, or text card. On-screen text at 0s, verbatim.
- [ ] Time until the topic is clear (seconds).
- [ ] Hook category: unexpected result / important consequence / surprising comparison / real-world demonstration / common misconception / useful discovery / genuine controversy.
- [ ] Number of cuts and any zoom or motion in 0–3s.

**C. Speaking style**
- [ ] Word count of full transcript ÷ duration = words per minute.
- [ ] Language mix: rough percent Hindi vs English words; which words stay English (tool names, numbers).
- [ ] Sentence length (average words per sentence); questions asked; second-person address ("aap/tum/you").
- [ ] Tone: calm, urgent, playful, sceptical. Filler words. Direct-to-camera or off-camera.

**D. Editing style**
- [ ] Total cuts; average shot length = duration ÷ cuts.
- [ ] Zoom/punch-in count; b-roll seconds vs face seconds; screen-recording seconds.
- [ ] Captions: present or not, words per group, lines, position (top/centre/lower third), highlight colour, animation, language/script (Latin vs Devanagari).
- [ ] Graphics: source logos, charts, citations on screen. Music bed and sound effects (count).
- [ ] Framing and background; whether the face stays visible during b-roll.

**E. Storytelling**
- [ ] Beat map with timestamps: hook, context, proof/demo, implication, close.
- [ ] Is there a source or citation, and where is it shown?
- [ ] Payoff timestamp; whether the end loops back to the opening.
- [ ] CTA type (follow, comment keyword, share, none) and timestamp.

**F. Performance ratios (compute, do not estimate)**
- [ ] Views ÷ followers (reach multiple).
- [ ] Likes ÷ views; comments ÷ views; shares ÷ views; saves ÷ views.
- [ ] Views relative to the creator's median of the last 20 posts (outlier score).
- [ ] For our own account only: 3-second hold rate, average watch time, completion rate, sends per reach, follows per reach (from Insights).

**Housekeeping**
- [ ] Re-read follower counts from the live profiles and overwrite the tracker estimates above.
